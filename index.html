Helpful information

Before you buy.

Are these current prices?

No. The September advertisements are used for this demo. Ask the store for current stock, currency and final price.

Can I pay on this website?

No. The enquiry basket prepares a message locally. It does not send orders, accept payments or reserve stock.

Are the photos of actual stock?

No. They are licensed reference or illustrative photos. Confirm the exact laptop model and condition.

What about warranty and software?

Ask the seller about warranty, battery testing, included accessories, operating system compatibility and legitimate software licences.
